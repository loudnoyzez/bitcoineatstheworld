What each thing has cost in sats, month by month since 2014. At every 4-year checkpoint since 2014, each of these cost fewer sats. Not every 4-year window, though: the bumps are Bitcoin's crashes, and each card shows how often the cost went the other way. History, not a forecast.

How to read this (the honest version)
  • It's mostly one fact, seen many ways. An item's price in sats is its dollar price divided by bitcoin's dollar price. When everything gets cheaper in sats, that's mostly bitcoin's price rising, not each item changing.
  • Fixed windows, no cherry-picking. The checkpoints are every 4 years from where the data starts (Sep 2014). Each card also counts every 4-year window where the cost went the other way.
  • Items are picked because you know them, not because they make bitcoin look good. None gets dropped for looking bad.
  • The bumps are crashes. When bitcoin falls, things suddenly cost more sats. That's shown, not hidden.
  • History, not a forecast. You still pay rent in your own currency, and bitcoin can fall 50% or more against it.
